There's a version of me who waits until everything is figured out. He has excellent plans. He has never shipped anything.

The other version starts while it still feels a bit embarrassing. The first draft is clumsy, the first version is held together with tape, and somehow that's the one that ends up teaching me what the thing should actually be.

## The part nobody tells you

Feeling ready is mostly a story you tell yourself afterwards. In the moment it just feels like doubt with a deadline.

- You learn more from one real user than from ten imagined ones.
- The plan changes the moment it meets anything real.
- Momentum is easier to steer than to start.

I'm restless writing this, which is probably the point. Restless is better than stuck.
