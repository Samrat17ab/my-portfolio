I woke up before the alarm today, which almost never happens. The light was that thin grey-blue it gets before the sun commits to anything, and for once I didn't reach for my phone.

It's strange how loud a quiet room can be when you actually listen to it. A kettle somewhere. A bird that sounded like it was asking a question. My own breathing, which I forget is there most days.

## What I noticed

I spend so much of the day reacting. A message comes in, I answer. A tab opens, I read. This morning had nothing to react to, and it felt less like emptiness and more like room.

> Maybe rest isn't the absence of things to do. Maybe it's the absence of things pulling at you.

I don't want to turn this into a routine I'll feel guilty about breaking. I just want to remember that the room is there.

---

Tomorrow the alarm will win again. That's fine. I know where the quiet is now.
